# TRACE: kahani ("Nishaan")

Season 1 ki story bible: kahani, kirdaar, aur ye endless game mein kaise khelegi.

---

## 1. Research se kya seekha

Ye cheezein maine asli scripts padh ke nikaali hain (The Fugitive, Catch Me If You Can, Heat, Enemy of the State, Bourne Identity), aur Drishyam, Kahaani aur Hades ke analysis se.

| Film / game | Kya seekha | TRACE mein kahan aayega |
|---|---|---|
| **The Fugitive** | Hero bekasoor hai. Chase ke neeche ek **mystery** chalti hai (asli qatil kaun?). Shikari Gerard ka credo hai *"Mujhe farak nahi padta, mera kaam pakadna hai"*. Baad mein hero phone pe yahi line yaad dilata hai, aur wahi call trace ho jaati hai. | Rana ka credo, phone pe baatcheet, chase ke neeche "sach ke tukde" ki mystery |
| **The Fugitive (dam scene)** | Hero bandh se koodta hai. Script likhti hai: *"Isne Gerard ko is bhagode ke baare mein kuch keemti bataya."* **Shikari har chaal se shikar ko seekhta hai.** | Rana tumhari aadatein seekhta hai (Nemesis system), ab kahani ka hissa |
| **Catch Me If You Can** | Har Christmas Eve Frank FBI agent ko phone karta hai. Agent kehta hai: *"Tumhare paas phone karne ko koi aur nahi hai."* Ant mein: *"Tum wapas aaoge, kyunki koi tumhara peecha nahi kar raha."* **Shikari aur shikar ek doosre ki zaroorat ban jaate hain.** | Har run ke baad "Rana ka call", aur Season ka ant |
| **Heat (diner scene)** | Cop aur chor ek table pe chai peete hain. Dono maante hain ki *"Mujhe aur kuch karna nahi aata."* Dushman ek doosre ka **aaina** hain. | Midpoint ka "Chai pe Rana" scene |
| **Enemy of the State** | Ek aam aadmi ke juute mein tracker, aur poori zindagi ke records uske khilaaf. Jeet tab hoti hai jab hero **unke hi aujaar unke khilaaf** use karta hai. | DRISHTI system. Player nakli nishaan banata hai (mechanic = theme) |
| **Bourne Identity** | *"Mujhe gaadiyon ke number yaad hain, par ye nahi pata main kaun hoon."* Hunar hai, pehchaan nahi. | "Chhaya" kaun hai? Har kirdaar ki apni kahani |
| **Drishyam** | Aam aadmi bills, tickets aur gawahon ki yaaddasht se **alibi banata hai**. Twist pehle se dikhaya gaya hota hai (thana ban raha tha). Parivaar ki raksha sabse bada daav. | Nishaan sach nahi, kahani hote hain. Twist ke saaf saaf clue |
| **Kahaani** | Heroine sabko istemaal karti hai. Sheher (Kolkata, Durga Puja) ek kirdaar hai. Twist "fair" hai, clue dialogue mein chhupe hain. | Handler "Didi" ka twist. Ganpati, monsoon, local train |
| **Hades** | Har baar marna ek kahani ka mauka hai. Kirdaar tumhari pichhli koshishon pe baat karte hain. Sach **thoda thoda** khulta hai. | Har run ke baad scene, har pakde jaane pe naya dialogue |
| **Shadow of Mordor (Nemesis)** | Dushman tumhe yaad rakhta hai ("Tumne mujhe jala diya tha!"). Isse game ki **apni kahaniyan** banti hain. | Rana ki diary, case board, police ke kirdaar jo tumhe yaad rakhte hain |

**Teen sabse bade sabak:**
1. **Chase ke neeche ek sawaal chahiye** jiska jawab player jaanna chahe. "Bhaago" kaafi nahi; "bhaago, *kyunki* sach dhoondhna hai" chahiye.
2. **Shikari, shikar jitna hi gehra hona chahiye.** Gerard, Hanratty aur Hanna ki wajah se hi in filmon ka chase dil ko chhoota hai.
3. **Theme ko khel mein badlo.** "Har kadam ek nishaan" ka asli twist ye hai ki **nishaan jhooth bhi bol sakte hain**, aur player khud nakli nishaan bana sakta hai.

---

## 2. Ek line mein (logline)

> Mumbai ka naya AI surveillance system **DRISHTI** har aadmi ka har kadam padhta hai: CCTV, UPI, phone. Jab ek aam aadmi ko DRISHTI ke **banaye hue nishanon** se qatil saabit kar diya jaata hai, to chhe aam log ek naam "**Chhaya**" ke peeche chhup kar sach bahar laane bhaagte hain. Unke peeche hai **Inspector Vikram Rana**, jo maanta hai ki *"data kabhi jhooth nahi bolta."*

**Theme:** *Har kadam ek nishaan chhodta hai. Par nishaan sach nahi hote. Nishaan sirf kahani hote hain, aur kahani jo likhta hai, wahi jeetta hai.*

## 3. Kirdaar

### Inspector Vikram Rana (shikari)
- 52 saal, Mumbai Crime Branch. Vidhur, ek beti **Pari (12)**, jo roz raat poochti hai *"Papa, aaj kisko pakda?"*
- 8 saal pehle uski biwi ka qatl hua tha. Qatil ek CCTV clip aur ek UPI receipt se pakda gaya. **Tab se Rana ka imaan data pe hai.**
- Credo (Gerard se): *"Tum bekasoor ho ya nahi, mera kaam nahi. Mera kaam tumhe pakadna hai."*
- Aadat: chai mein do chammach cheeni, purani diary, aur har nishaan pe laal dhaaga.
- **Uska arc:** "Data jhooth nahi bolta" se "Maine ek bekasoor ko jail bheja tha" tak. (Twist dekho, section 6.)

### "Chhaya" (bhagoda)
Rana ki file mein Chhaya ek insaan hai. Asal mein **chhe log** hain, jo aapas mein ek doosre ki madad karte hain aur baari baari bhaagte hain. **Yahi game ka character select hai.** Har kirdaar ka DRISHTI se apna zakhm hai (Drishyam wala parivaar ka daav):

| Kirdaar | Kaun | DRISHTI ne kya chheena |
|---|---|---|
| **Meera** (Agent) | Ex-RAW analyst | Bhai **Kunal** jail mein hai, DRISHTI ke nakli CCTV se "danga bhadkaane" ke ilzaam mein |
| **Arjun** (Driver) | Kaali-peeli taxi driver | Uski taxi ka GPS ek qatl ki raat "maukay pe" dikhaya gaya. Licence chheen liya gaya |
| **Anjali** (Hacker) | DRISHTI ki purani engineer | Usi ne DRISHTI ka face-match likha tha. **Guilt hi uski chaal hai** |
| **Kabir** (Con-man) | Chhota thug, bada dil | Use paisa chahiye tha, par Kunal uska bachpan ka dost hai |
| **Kavya** (Actress) | TV serial actress | Uska deepfake video viral karke use "terror funding" mein phansaya gaya |
| **Rani** (Ex-Army) | Retired havaldar | Fauji pension rokne ke liye DRISHTI ne use "desh-drohi" bana diya |

### "Didi" (handler)
- Sirf phone pe milti hai: WhatsApp messages aur dhundhli awaaz. Chhaya ko tips deti hai: "Gate 72 mat lena", "Dhabe ke peeche truck hai".
- Pyaari, samajhdaar, hamesha sahi. **Twist ka kendra** (section 6).

### DRISHTI aur Nayan Systems
- Private company **Nayan Systems** ne sarkar ke liye sheher-bhar ka AI bana diya: har CCTV, har UPI, har mobile tower.
- Uska chhupa feature **"Rachna"** (srijan) nakli nishaan **bana** sakta hai: CCTV frame mein chehra badalna, UPI log mein entry jodna, tower ping khiskana.
- Iska maalik koi chehra nahi hai. Bas ek "Board", aur ek mantri ke beti-damaad.

### Chhote kirdaar (har run mein milte hain)
- **Pari:** Rana ki beti. Rana ke phone call ke peeche uski awaaz. Season ke ant mein ahem.
- **Ismail bhai:** Goa docks ka crew-pass wala.
- **Chaiwala Bablu:** har station pe milne wala chaiwala (mazaak, aur kabhi kabhi khabar).
- **Constable Shinde:** Rana ka naya, ghabraya hua saathi. Player usse baar baar takrata hai. Nemesis jaisa: *"Phir tum! Pichhli baar tumne mujhe dart maara tha!"*

## 4. Season 1 ki kahani (5 adhyaay)

Ye kahani **runs ke beech** thodi thodi khulegi (Hades jaisa). Har adhyaay tab khulega jab player kuch milestone poore karega (escape, ya "sach ke tukde" ikatthe karna).

### Adhyaay 1: "Visarjan" (shuruaat, pehla run)
- **Opening (cold open):** Ganpati visarjan ki raat, Girgaon Chowpatty, dhol, bheed. Meera ko ek pen drive milti hai jisme DRISHTI ka "Rachna" feature hai, aur saath mein Kunal ki CCTV clip **jo kabhi hui hi nahi**.
- Tabhi phone pe news: *"Chowpatty pe guard ka qatl. CCTV mein sandigdh: ek aurat, laal dupatta."* Meera ka laal dupatta. **DRISHTI ne qatl ke 2 minute baad hi uske nishaan bana diye.**
- Didi ka pehla message: *"Tumhare paas 6 ghante hain. Mumbai se nikal ke Goa ke jahaz tak pahuncho. Ek kadam galat, ek nishaan zyada, aur Rana tumhare darwaze pe."*
- **Rana ka intro:** apne flat mein Pari ko sula raha hai, phone bajta hai. *"Chhaya. Naya naam. Chalo, dekhte hain kitne nishaan chhodta hai."*

**Gameplay:** ye wahi Mumbai → Goa wala run hai jo abhi game mein hai, par ab har kadam ke peeche kahani hai.

### Adhyaay 2: "Pehla nishaan" (shikari seekhta hai)
- Har run ke baad **"Rana ka call"** (Catch Me If You Can jaisa). Chhaya Rana ko phone karti hai, ya Rana Chhaya ko.
  - Rana tumhare run ki asli baatein bolta hai: *"Tumne Dadar pe UPI se chai li. Chhaya, chai ke ₹20 ke liye apna pata de diya?"*
  - Ye **Nemesis system ki awaaz** hai. Har run ke baad Rana ki line player ke asli khel se banti hai.
- Har run mein ek **"sach ka tukda"** chhupa hota hai: server room, CCTV control room, kisi gawah ki baat. Inse dheere dheere pata chalta hai ki DRISHTI ne **kitne logon** ko phansaya.
- Har kirdaar ki apni chhoti kahani khulti hai: Arjun ki taxi, Kavya ka deepfake.

### Adhyaay 3: "Chai pe Rana" (midpoint, aaina, Heat + Fugitive)
- Ek scripted run: Rana Chhaya ko Madgaon station pe gher leta hai. **Do raaste:**
  - Bhaago (Fugitive ki dam wali chhalang: chalti train ke neeche se paar).
  - Ya **ruk jao**. Rana kehta hai: *"Chai pilaoge?"* Platform ki bench pe dono baithe hain, aas-paas aam log (Heat ka diner).
- Baatcheet ka core:
  - Chhaya: *"Maine kisi ko nahi maara."*
  - Rana: *"Mujhe farak nahi padta."*
  - Chhaya: *"Tumhari biwi ka qatil bhi CCTV se pakda gaya tha na? Kabhi socha, wo bhi data tha?"*
  - Rana chup ho jaata hai. **Pehli baar uski aankh mein shak.**
- Rana phir bhi pakadne uthta hai. Chhaya bhaag jaati hai, ya pakdi jaati hai (bail mechanic).
- **Midpoint mod:** iske baad Rana ke calls badal jaate hain. Wo poochne lagta hai: *"Ye 'Rachna' kya hai?"*

### Adhyaay 4: "Rachna" (sab bikhar jaata hai)
- Chhaya ko pata chalta hai ki **Rana ki biwi ka case DRISHTI ka pehla test tha.** Jo "qatil" pakda gaya, wo bekasoor tha. Asli qatil ko DRISHTI ne bachaya, kyunki wo Nayan Systems ka investor tha.
- Rana ka sabse bura pal: jis data pe uska imaan tha, usi ne use jhootha banaya. Pari phone pe: *"Papa, aap ro rahe ho?"*
- Usi waqt **Didi ka twist khulta hai** (section 6). Chhaya akeli pad jaati hai.
- Player ko lagta hai sab haar gaye. ("All is lost": shikari bhi toota hua, aur saathi bhi dhokhebaaz.)

### Adhyaay 5: "Sabse bada nishaan" (climax)
- Chhaya ek faisla leti hai: *"Agar nishaan kahani hain, to hum sabse badi kahani likhenge."*
- **Final run:** Nayan Systems ka data centre (Navi Mumbai), Ganpati ke aakhri din, sheher bheed mein.
  - Player ko **DRISHTI ke apne aujaar** (Rachna) se nakli nishaan banane hain. Police ko galat jagah bhejna hai, khud asli jagah pahunchna hai (Enemy of the State ka climax).
- **Rana ka faisla:** wo data centre ke bahar khada hai. Pakde ya rasta de?
  - Agar player ne pichhle runs mein Rana ke saath "imaandari" dikhayi hai (koi qatl nahi, kam goli), to Rana kehta hai: *"Jao. Main kehdunga maine nishaan nahi dekha. Pehli baar."*
  - Agar player ne bahut khoon bahaya hai, to Rana: *"Tum unhi jaise ho gaye."* Aur aakhri chase hota hai.
- **Endings (3):**
  1. **"Sach ka nishaan"** (best): pura Rachna data live leak. Kunal aur baaki sab riha. Rana TV pe gawahi deta hai.
  2. **"Chhaya"**: data Rana ko de diya. Wo usse sahi raaste pe le jaata hai, par Chhaya ko desh chhodna padta hai.
  3. **"Naya Rachna"**: data Didi ko bech diya. Paisa mila, sab barbaad. Season 2 ka dark start.

### Season ka ant (Catch Me If You Can ka ant)
- Kuch mahine baad. Rana Chhaya ko phone karta hai: *"Ek naya system aaya hai, Hyderabad mein. Mujhe ek aisa insaan chahiye jo nishaan padhna aur banana dono jaanta ho."*
- Chhaya: *"Tum mere peeche bhaag rahe ho, ya mere saath?"*
- Rana: *"Pata nahi. Par tum aaoge. Kyunki ab koi tumhara peecha nahi kar raha."*
- **Season 2 shuru:** Rana handler, Chhaya bhagodi, naya sheher, naya dushman. **Isi se game kabhi khatam nahi hota.**

## 5. Endless game mein kahani kaise chalegi

Hades + Nemesis ka mix. Teen parten:

**A. Har run (procedural, har baar alag)**
- Har run ek safar hai (abhi: Mumbai → Goa). Adhyaay ke hisaab se sheher badlenge: Mumbai → Goa, Delhi → Jaipur, Kolkata → Sundarbans…
- Zone, police aur events random. Har run mein **ek sach ka tukda** chhupa hai, mile ya na mile.

**B. Runs ke beech (authored, thoda thoda)**
- **Rana ka call** (30 second): ek comic panel aur 2–3 line, tumhare run se bani hui:
  - Tumne goli chalayi: *"Chhaya, aaj tumne ek constable ko ghayal kiya. Uski beti bhi Pari jitni hai."*
  - Tum bina nishaan nikle: *"Koi nishaan nahi. Ya to tum bhoot ho, ya DRISHTI mein ched hai. Dono baatein mujhe pareshaan karti hain."*
  - Tum pakde gaye: *"Bail de di. Phir milenge. Tum hamesha UPI se bhaagti ho, Chhaya."*
- **Safehouse** (Hades ka House): chhe kirdaar ek purani chawl mein. Har baar ek do line nayi baatcheet. Unki apni kahaniyan dheere khulti hain.
- **Rana ka case board:** ab ye kahani ka hissa hai. Board pe tumhari photos aur saath mein DRISHTI ke nakli nishaan. Adhyaay 4 mein Rana khud board pe "**NAKLI?**" likhta hai.

**C. Milestones se adhyaay khulte hain**

| Adhyaay | Kaise khulega |
|---|---|
| 1 | Pehla run (tutorial) |
| 2 | Pehli baar Goa pahuncho |
| 3 | 4 sach ke tukde |
| 4 | Midpoint run poora (chai ya chalang) |
| 5 | 10 tukde, aur kam se kam 2 kirdaar ki kahani poori |

## 6. Twist: "Didi" kaun hai? (Kahaani style, fair play)

**Sach:** Didi = **Dr. Sunanda Iyer**, DRISHTI ki creator, Nayan Systems se nikaali gayi. Wo Chhaya ko istemaal kar rahi hai: Nayan se Rachna ka **master key** chura ke khud bechna chahti hai. Goa ka jahaz bachne ka raasta nahi tha. **Jahaz pe uske kharidaar baithe the.**

**Clue jo pehle se dikhenge** (player ko baad mein "arre, ye to pehle hi dikha tha!" lagna chahiye):
1. Didi ke tips hamesha sahi hote hain, **itne sahi** ki koi insaan itna nahi jaan sakta. (Wo DRISHTI ka live feed dekh rahi hai.)
2. Didi ke message **hamesha CCTV ke dekhne ke 5 second baad** aate hain. (Wo bhi wahi camera dekh rahi hai.)
3. Didi tumhe hamesha **Gate 72 / Docks / ek khaas camera** ke paas se le jaati hai. (Wo DRISHTI ka blind spot test kar rahi hai.)
4. Pehle hi run mein Ismail bhai kehta hai: *"Is jahaz pe crew kam, mehmaan zyada hain."*
5. Anjali (hacker) ek baar kehti hai: *"DRISHTI ka face-match maine likha tha, par 'Rachna' kisi aur ka kaam tha. Ek aurat thi, sab use Didi bulate the."*
6. Didi kabhi video call nahi karti. Uski awaaz ke peeche hamesha **server fan** ki halki awaaz hai.

**Reveal ka pal (Adhyaay 4):** Goa docks pe jahaz ka darwaza khulta hai. Andar Didi, aur Nayan ke kharidaar. *"Shukriya, Meera. Tumne meri kahani bahut achhe se likhi."*

## 7. Teen namune ke scene

### Scene 1: Cold open (Adhyaay 1, game ka pehla minute)
> **EXT. GIRGAON CHOWPATTY: RAAT.** Dhol. Laakhon log. Ganpati samundar ki taraf.
> Meera bheed mein, haath mein pen drive. Phone vibrate hota hai.
> **NEWS ALERT:** *"Chowpatty pe guard ka qatl. CCTV mein sandigdh: laal dupatta."*
> Meera apne dupatte ko dekhti hai. Laal. Camera ki laal batti uski taraf ghoomti hai.
> **DIDI (msg):** *Bhaago. Abhi.*
> → **Gameplay shuru**, bheed ke beech, dhol ki taal pe.

### Scene 2: Rana ka call (har run ke baad, run ke data se bana)
> **INT. RANA KA FLAT: RAAT.** Pari so rahi hai. Rana diary mein laal dhaaga jodta hai.
> Phone bajta hai. Unknown number.
> **RANA:** Chhaya. Teesri baar. Tumhe pata hai, aaj tumne Dadar pe kitne nishaan chhode? *(diary dekhta hai)* Saat. Ek UPI, do camera, aur ek chaiwala jo tumhara hulia yaad rakhta hai.
> **CHHAYA:** Aur tumne kitne sach dekhe, Inspector?
> **RANA:** *(chup)* Mujhe sach nahi chahiye. Mujhe tum chahiye.
> *(Click. Kahin door, Pari ki awaaz: "Papa, kaun tha?")*

### Scene 3: Chai pe Rana (midpoint)
> **EXT. MADGAON STATION: RAAT.** Platform ki bench. Do kulhad chai. Aas-paas aam log, chaiwala Bablu, ek soya hua coolie.
> **RANA:** Saat saal se main data pe jeeta hoon. Data ne meri biwi ka qatil pakda.
> **CHHAYA:** Aur agar data ne galat aadmi pakda ho?
> **RANA:** *(kulhad neeche rakhta hai)* To main kis cheez pe jeeta raha?
> *(Train ki siti.)*
> **RANA:** Uth jao, Chhaya. Mujhe farak nahi padta tum sach bol rahi ho ya nahi.
> **CHHAYA:** Padta hai. Isiliye tumne abhi tak hathkadi nahi nikaali.
> → **Player ka faisla:** chalti train ke neeche se bhaago, ya hath aage karo.

## 8. Game mein kya badalna hoga (kahani ke liye)

1. **Har run ke baad "Rana ka call" screen.** 3 line, tumhare run ke data se. (Pehla aur sabse sasta, sabse bada asar.)
2. **Cold open scene** pehle run se pehle: visarjan, laal dupatta, news alert.
3. **"Sach ka tukda"** har run mein ek chhupi jagah, aur safehouse mein ek "sach ki deewar".
4. **Didi ke messages** phone pe (abhi ke handler tips Didi ban jaayenge), saath mein clue 1–3 ka timing.
5. **Character select = Chhaya ke chhe log**, har ek ki ek line ki backstory, aur unlock hone wali kahani.
6. **Nakli nishaan mechanic:** jaan boojh ke galat jagah UPI, ya phone kisi bus mein chhod dena. Theme yahi hai, aur ye kisi doosre game mein nahi.
7. Bina wajah ke random popups hatao. Har event kahani se jude: baraat = visarjan, gaddaar = Didi ka test.
